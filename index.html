About Tim

Tim A. Schultz has spent more than 25 years creating business-to-business marketing communications that sell the product first.

He spent 15 years as an international marketing communications director for industrial manufacturers, and more than ten as Vice-President, Creative at a B2B agency. Today he runs Marcom Solutions and edits The Bratenahl Lamplighter.
